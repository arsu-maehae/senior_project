# 009 — Keep Security 4688 (process creation)

**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Status:** accepted · **Task:** T3

## Context
Spec 01 §8 left open whether to keep Security 4688. Owner rule (2026-11-09): keep it if the data-checker finds it in Security. The data-checker counted it in every dry-run trial (`events_dedup`, Security, 4688):

| Trial | dc01 | ep-01 | ep-02 | ep-03 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| s01 (target side only) | 223 | 342 | 909 | 658 |
| s02 | 223 | 342 | 318 | – |
| s03 | 651 | 278 | – | 4139 |
| s04 | 400 | 257 | 305 | – |
| s05 | 563 | 597 | 484 | 4320 |
| s06 | 440 | 288 | 478 | 4157 |
| s07 | 472 | 319 | 502 | – |

Every 4688 has a non-NULL `session_id` (taken from SubjectLogonId, NF-11). Sysmon 1 also has `session_id` on every row. s01 dc01 and ep-01 are S02 exports (decision 001), so s01 and s02 share those counts.

## Options considered
| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| A. Keep 4688 | A second process-creation source with the creator's logon session; useful where Sysmon 1 is missing | More rows (ep-03 has about 4,000 per trial) |
| B. Drop 4688 | Smaller DB | Loses the only process source on hosts or windows without Sysmon 1 |

## Decision
Option A. 4688 is in `TRIAL_RELEVANT_EVENT_IDS` (NF-09).

## Consequences
ep-03 contributes thousands of 4688 rows in s03, s05 and s06; indexes keep queries fast (s05 imports in about 25 s). The gate does not use 4688 as a trigger.

## Kill criteria
If T6/T7 never read a 4688 row on the development trials, drop it from the whitelist to shrink the DB.

## Committee answer
**EN:** We keep Security 4688 because it is present in every trial and carries the creator's logon session, a second source next to Sysmon 1.
**TH:** เราเก็บ Security 4688 เพราะมีในทุก trial และบอก logon session ของผู้สร้างโปรเซส เป็นแหล่งที่สองคู่กับ Sysmon 1
